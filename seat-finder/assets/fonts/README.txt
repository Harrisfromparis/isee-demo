Self-hosted fonts (no third-party requests). Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1;
the licence texts are in this folder. Files are the "latin" subset woff2 files from the
@fontsource/poppins and @fontsource/atkinson-hyperlegible npm packages (version 5.3.0),
which cover English, Irish (fadas) and the punctuation ISEE uses.
  Poppins 600 and 700 (headings), Atkinson Hyperlegible 400 and 700 (body).
