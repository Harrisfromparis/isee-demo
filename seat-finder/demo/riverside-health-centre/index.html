Route demos

Try the route demo: Riverside Health Centre

This is the Find your way part of iSEE. This demo is one example: finding reception, a clinic room or a department in a made-up health centre. Other kinds of places and destinations will follow. Plan a route to a place. Choose how to follow it: one step at a time or as an overview, with or without stairs. Nothing here is a real place.

This place is made up

Riverside Health Centre is fictional. Its signs, routes and places are invented for the demo. They say nothing about any real building.

How the demo works

  1. Choose where you are going. Pick a place from the list, which is grouped the way a department list would be: reception, GP clinic, therapies, tests, toilets, café and more.
  2. Choose where you are. Pick an entrance or another start point. Each one names the sign to look for.
  3. Follow the route. Read one step at a time. Confirm each checkpoint when you reach it. You can go back a step.
  4. Choose stairs or no stairs. Step-free routes use the lift. They never use stairs, or paths that have not been checked.
  5. Say when you have arrived. At the last step, iSEE asks "Have you arrived?" and waits for you to answer.

The made-up health centre has one passage that is closed, a side door with one step, and a side-door shortcut whose step-free status is unknown, so you can see how each is handled.

The venue guide lists the closed and unchecked paths.

What the demo does not do

  • It does not use your phone's sensors. You confirm each checkpoint yourself.
  • It does not connect to any real venue.
  • It has not been tested in a real building, or by people using screen readers. See Designed for everyone.

Your choices stay in this browser so a refresh does not lose your place. Privacy explains this and how to clear them. You can also try the other demo.

The sample route in words

This is the route the planner gives for going to Physiotherapy (department, First floor), starting from Main entrance. It is the same text, made when this page was built. You can read it without JavaScript.

Standard route

Overview

  1. Going to Physiotherapy, First floor.
  2. Start at Main entrance.
  3. Take the stairs to the first floor.
  4. Look for Physiotherapy.

Every step

  1. Go through the main doors. They open by themselves. Continue straight ahead into the foyer, until you can see the long desk with the sign RECEPTION. Look or listen for: Sign: RECEPTION. Large letters above the long desk. The desk has a hearing loop sign on the front. Next checkpoint: Main foyer. The open foyer just inside the main doors. A long desk with the sign RECEPTION is on one side and a tall screen with the words SELF CHECK-IN is on the other.
  2. Continue straight through the foyer and along the wide passage. It leads to the blue chairs of waiting area A. A fish tank is set into the wall. Look or listen for: Fish tank. A tank set into the wall of waiting area A. It makes a quiet bubbling sound. Next checkpoint: Waiting area A. A wide area with blue chairs in rows. The sign says WAITING AREA A. A fish tank on the wall hums quietly.
  3. Go along the corridor on the east side of the blue chairs, until you reach the hanging signs for LIFT, STAIRS, PHARMACY and X-RAY. Look or listen for: Hanging signs: LIFT, STAIRS, PHARMACY, X-RAY. Four signs hanging from the ceiling at the east corridor junction. Next checkpoint: East corridor junction. A corridor junction. Hanging signs point to LIFT, STAIRS, PHARMACY and X-RAY.
  4. Follow the sign for STAIRS. The staircase is at the end of the passage, beside the lift passage. Look or listen for: Sign: STAIRS TO FIRST FLOOR. Wall sign in large letters at the foot of the staircase. Next checkpoint: Foot of the stairs. A staircase with a handrail on both sides. The sign says STAIRS TO FIRST FLOOR.
  5. Go up the stairs to the first floor. There are 22 steps with one landing halfway. Handrails are on both sides. Look or listen for: Sign: STAIRS TO FIRST FLOOR. Wall sign in large letters at the foot of the staircase. Next checkpoint: Top of the stairs. The landing at the top of the stairs. The sign says FIRST FLOOR.
  6. From the top of the stairs, continue onto the wide landing. Stop at the wall sign that lists THERAPIES, BLOOD TESTS, WAITING AREA B and TOILETS. Look or listen for: Sign: FIRST FLOOR directory. A wall sign on the landing that lists THERAPIES, BLOOD TESTS, WAITING AREA B and TOILETS. Next checkpoint: First floor landing. A wide landing between the stairs and the lift. A sign lists THERAPIES, BLOOD TESTS, WAITING AREA B and TOILETS.
  7. Follow the sign for WAITING AREA B along the first floor corridor, until you reach the corridor junction with signs for WAITING AREA B and TOILETS. Look or listen for: Sign: WAITING AREA B. A hanging sign above the green chairs. Next checkpoint: First floor corridor. The corridor along the first floor. Signs point both ways to WAITING AREA B and TOILETS.
  8. Continue a short way to the green chairs under the sign WAITING AREA B. Look or listen for: Sign: WAITING AREA B. A hanging sign above the green chairs. Next checkpoint: Waiting area B. An area with green chairs along the walls. The sign says WAITING AREA B. Rooms open off this area.
  9. From the green chairs, go to the wide double doors with the word PHYSIOTHERAPY. Next checkpoint: Physiotherapy. Wide double doors with the word PHYSIOTHERAPY and a picture of a figure stretching.
  10. You have reached the wide double doors with the word PHYSIOTHERAPY and a picture of a figure stretching. Waiting area B is the nearest place to sit.
