Try the route demo: Harbour Theatre

This is the Find your way part of iSEE. This demo is one example: a made-up theatre, finding a seat. Other kinds of places and destinations will follow. Plan a route to a seat. Choose how to follow it: one step at a time or as an overview, with or without stairs. Nothing here is a real place.

This venue is made up

Harbour Theatre is a fictional venue. Its signs, routes, seats and shows are invented for the demo. They say nothing about any real building.

Plan a route

Simulated phone sensors — demonstration only

How the demo works

  1. Choose a seat. Pick a section, row and seat in the made-up theatre.
  2. Choose where you are. Pick an entrance or another start point. Each one names the sign to look for.
  3. Follow the route. Read one step at a time. Confirm each checkpoint when you reach it. You can go back a step.
  4. Choose stairs or no stairs. Step-free routes never use stairs. They also avoid paths that have not been checked.

The made-up venue has one passage that is closed and one ramp whose step-free status is unknown, so you can see how both are handled. The venue guide lists them.

What the demo does not do

  • It does not use your phone's sensors. You confirm each checkpoint yourself.
  • It does not connect to any real venue.
  • It has not been tested in a real building, or by people using screen readers. See Designed for everyone.

Your choices stay in this browser so a refresh does not lose your place. Privacy explains this and how to clear them.

The sample route in words

This is the route the planner gives for the made-up sample ticket (Section B, Row C, Seat 12), starting from Main entrance, Quay Street. It is the same text, made when this page was built. You can read it without JavaScript.

Standard route

Overview

  1. Going to Section B, Upper level, Row C, Seat 12.
  2. Start at Main entrance, Quay Street.
  3. Take the stairs to the upper level.
  4. Go through Door B West, Section B.
  5. Find Row C.
  6. Find Seat 12.

Every step

  1. Go through the main doors. They open by themselves. Walk straight ahead into the lower lobby, until you are under the large round clock. Look or listen for: Large round clock. On the wall facing the main doors, above head height. It ticks loudly. Next checkpoint: Lower lobby, under the clock. The open lobby with a large round clock on the wall. The clock ticks loudly.
  2. Facing the clock, turn left. Walk to the wide staircase with the sign STAIRS TO UPPER LEVEL. Look or listen for: Sign: STAIRS TO UPPER LEVEL. Hanging sign in large white letters, to the left of the clock. Next checkpoint: Foot of the main stairs. Wide staircase with a handrail on both sides. The sign says STAIRS TO UPPER LEVEL.
  3. Go up the main stairs to the upper level. There are 24 steps with one landing halfway. Handrails are on both sides. Look or listen for: Sign: STAIRS TO UPPER LEVEL. Hanging sign in large white letters, to the left of the clock. Next checkpoint: Top of the main stairs. Landing at the top of the main stairs. The sign says UPPER LEVEL.
  4. From the top of the stairs, walk straight ahead onto the carpeted concourse. Stop at the programme stand. Look or listen for: Programme stand. Waist-high wooden stand at the west end of the concourse. Next checkpoint: Upper concourse, west end. Carpeted concourse. A programme stand is here. Signs point to DOOR A and DOOR B WEST.
  5. From the programme stand, go to the nearest doors. They say B WEST. Go through Door B West. Next checkpoint: Door B West, Section B. Double doors with the words B WEST.
  6. Walk down the stepped aisle. There is one step at each row. Row E is the first row you reach, on your right. Each row has its letter on the end seat. Next checkpoint: Section B, Row E, west aisle end. Row letter on the end of the row, raised and in large print.
  7. Keep going down the stepped aisle. There is one step at each row to the next row. This is Row D, the 2nd row from the doors, on your right. Next checkpoint: Section B, Row D, west aisle end. Row letter on the end of the row, raised and in large print.
  8. Keep going down the stepped aisle. There is one step at each row to the next row. This is Row C, the 3rd row from the doors, on your right. Next checkpoint: Section B, Row C, west aisle end. Row letter on the end of the row, raised and in large print.
  9. Turn right into Row C. Seat numbers go down as you walk along the row, starting at 14. Seat 12 is the 3rd seat. The number is on the seat back.

Step-free route

Overview

  1. Going to Section B, Upper level, Row C, Seat 12.
  2. Start at Main entrance, Quay Street.
  3. Take the lift to the upper level.
  4. Go through Door B East, Section B.
  5. Find Row C.
  6. Find Seat 12.

Every step

  1. Go through the main doors. They open by themselves. Walk straight ahead into the lower lobby, until you are under the large round clock. Look or listen for: Large round clock. On the wall facing the main doors, above head height. It ticks loudly. Next checkpoint: Lower lobby, under the clock. The open lobby with a large round clock on the wall. The clock ticks loudly.
  2. Facing the clock, turn right. Walk along the corridor until you reach the hanging sign for LIFT and EAST STAIRS. Look or listen for: Sign: LIFT. Hanging sign with the word LIFT and a lift symbol. Also in braille on the wall plate beside the call button. Next checkpoint: East corridor. Corridor junction. A hanging sign points to LIFT and EAST STAIRS.
  3. At the hanging sign, turn towards LIFT. The lift is at the end of the short passage. Look or listen for: Sign: LIFT. Hanging sign with the word LIFT and a lift symbol. Also in braille on the wall plate beside the call button. Next checkpoint: Lift, ground floor. One lift with a call button at 1 metre height. The sign says LIFT TO UPPER LEVEL. The lift announces each floor.
  4. Take the lift to the upper level. Press the button marked 1. The lift says "Upper level" when it arrives. Look or listen for: Sign: LIFT. Hanging sign with the word LIFT and a lift symbol. Also in braille on the wall plate beside the call button. Next checkpoint: Lift, upper level. Lift doors on the upper level. The sign says LIFT TO GROUND FLOOR.
  5. Leave the lift and walk straight ahead onto the carpeted concourse. Stop at the water fountain. You may hear it humming. Look or listen for: Water fountain. Wall-mounted fountain at the east end of the concourse. It makes a steady hum. Next checkpoint: Upper concourse, east end. Carpeted concourse. A water fountain is here and it hums. A sign points to DOOR B EAST.
  6. From the water fountain, go to the nearest doors. They say B EAST. Go through Door B East. The floor is level. Next checkpoint: Door B East, Section B. Double doors with the words B EAST. The floor is level through the doors.
  7. Walk down the sloping aisle. There are no steps. Row E is the first row you reach, on your left. Each row has its letter on the end seat. Next checkpoint: Section B, Row E, east aisle end. Row letter on the end of the row, raised and in large print.
  8. Keep going down the sloping aisle. There are no steps to the next row. This is Row D, the 2nd row from the doors, on your left. Next checkpoint: Section B, Row D, east aisle end. Row letter on the end of the row, raised and in large print.
  9. Keep going down the sloping aisle. There are no steps to the next row. This is Row C, the 3rd row from the doors, on your left. Next checkpoint: Section B, Row C, east aisle end. Row letter on the end of the row, raised and in large print.
  10. Turn left into Row C. Seat numbers go up as you walk along the row, starting at 1. Seat 12 is the 12th seat. The number is on the seat back.
