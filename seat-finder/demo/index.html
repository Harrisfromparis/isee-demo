What a demo does

  1. Choose where you are going. A seat, or a place such as a reception desk, a clinic room or a department.
  2. Choose where you are. Pick an entrance or another start point. Each one names the sign to look for.
  3. Follow the route. Read one step at a time. Confirm each checkpoint when you reach it.
  4. Choose stairs or no stairs. Step-free routes never use stairs. They also avoid paths that have not been checked.

What the demos do not do

  • They do not use your phone's sensors. You confirm each checkpoint yourself.
  • They do not connect to any real venue.
  • They have not been tested in a real building, or by people using screen readers. See Designed for everyone.
