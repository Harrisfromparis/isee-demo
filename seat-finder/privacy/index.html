Privacy

Privacy

This page says what this website does with your information. The short version: it keeps almost nothing, and it keeps that on your own device.

What this website does

  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • There are no cookies.
  • There is no analytics and no tracking.
  • After a page has loaded, it makes no further network requests. Nothing is sent anywhere. Fonts and pictures come from this website, not from anyone else.
  • This website does not ask for permission to use your location, camera, Bluetooth or motion sensors.

What stays in your browser

When you use the route planner, your choices are kept in this browser tab's session storage, on your device. They are your seat, your start point, your route options and the step you are on. This is so a refresh does not lose your place. Closing the tab ends the session and the browser forgets them. They are not sent to anyone.

Your ticket and route details are never put in the page address, so they do not end up in history, bookmarks or shared links.

Clear what is saved

This removes the route choices that this website saved in this browser. It does not touch anything else.

The planned phone apps

Not available yet. The phone apps have not been released and have not been tested on phones or in venues. If they are released, they would ask for:

  • your location, to help follow your progress;
  • your phone's motion sensors, for the same reason;
  • the camera, only for an optional camera-assisted pilot that you would have to switch on yourself.

Each of these would be a choice. Confirming checkpoints by hand would always stay available. This page will be updated, with details, before any app asks for anything.
