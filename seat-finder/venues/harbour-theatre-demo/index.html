Entrances and start points

These are the places you can say you are starting from. Each one has a sign or feature to look for.

Entrances

  • Main entrance, Quay Street Ground floor
    Look for: Glass doors under the canopy. The words HARBOUR THEATRE are above the doors.
  • East entrance, Lantern Lane Ground floor
    Look for: Single door beside the box office window. The sign above it says EAST ENTRANCE.

Other start points

  • Lower lobby, under the clock Ground floor
    Look for: The open lobby with a large round clock on the wall. The clock ticks loudly.
  • Lift, ground floor Ground floor
    Look for: One lift with a call button at 1 metre height. The sign says LIFT TO UPPER LEVEL. The lift announces each floor.
  • Upper concourse, west end Upper level
    Look for: Carpeted concourse. A programme stand is here. Signs point to DOOR A and DOOR B WEST.
  • Upper concourse, east end Upper level
    Look for: Carpeted concourse. A water fountain is here and it hums. A sign points to DOOR B EAST.

Seating

Main house, standard seating. Made-up events in this layout: Harbour Lights (made-up show), evening; Harbour Lights (made-up show), relaxed matinee.

Section A

  • Rows: D, C, B, A.
  • Seat numbers: 1 to 8.
  • From the aisle end of each row in Section A, seat numbers go up as you walk along the row. The first seat you reach is seat 1.
  • Row D: the directions stop at the start of the row, because the seat numbers in that row have not been checked. Read the numbers on the seat backs or talk to venue staff.

Section B

  • Rows: E, D, C, B, A.
  • Seat numbers: 1 to 14.
  • From the west aisle end of each row in Section B, seat numbers go down as you walk along the row. The first seat you reach is seat 14.
  • From the east aisle end of each row in Section B, seat numbers go up as you walk along the row. The first seat you reach is seat 1.

Stairs and lifts

  • Stairs, Ground floor to Upper level. Not step-free
    Go up the main stairs to the upper level. There are 24 steps with one landing halfway. Handrails are on both sides.
  • Stairs, Ground floor to Upper level. Not step-free
    Go up the east stairs to the upper level. There are 26 steps. The handrail is on the right.
  • Lift, Ground floor to Upper level. Step-free in the demo data
    Take the lift to the upper level. Press the button marked 1. The lift says "Upper level" when it arrives.

Landmarks

These are signs and features that are already in the building.

  • Large round clock Ground floor
    On the wall facing the main doors, above head height. It ticks loudly.
  • Sign: STAIRS TO UPPER LEVEL Ground floor
    Hanging sign in large white letters, to the left of the clock.
  • Sign: LIFT Ground floor
    Hanging sign with the word LIFT and a lift symbol. Also in braille on the wall plate beside the call button.
  • Sign: EAST STAIRS Ground floor
    Wall sign in large letters at the foot of the narrow staircase.
  • Programme stand Upper level
    Waist-high wooden stand at the west end of the concourse.
  • Water fountain Upper level
    Wall-mounted fountain at the east end of the concourse. It makes a steady hum.

Talking to venue staff

In this made-up venue, staff in dark green jackets are at the box office in the lower lobby and at each auditorium door. Anyone can talk to them.
