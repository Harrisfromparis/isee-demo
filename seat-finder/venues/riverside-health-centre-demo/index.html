Entrances and start points

These are the places you can say you are starting from. Each one has a sign or feature to look for.

Entrances

  • Main entrance Ground floor
    Look for: Automatic glass doors under a green canopy. The words RIVERSIDE HEALTH CENTRE are above the doors.
  • Side entrance Ground floor
    Look for: A single door on the east side of the building. The sign above it says SIDE ENTRANCE. There is one step up at the door.

Other start points

  • Main foyer Ground floor
    Look for: The open foyer just inside the main doors. A long desk with the sign RECEPTION is on one side and a tall screen with the words SELF CHECK-IN is on the other.
  • Waiting area A Ground floor
    Look for: A wide area with blue chairs in rows. The sign says WAITING AREA A. A fish tank on the wall hums quietly.
  • East corridor junction Ground floor
    Look for: A corridor junction. Hanging signs point to LIFT, STAIRS, PHARMACY and X-RAY.
  • Lift, ground floor Ground floor
    Look for: One lift with a call button at a low height. The sign says LIFT. The lift says each floor aloud.
  • First floor landing First floor
    Look for: A wide landing between the stairs and the lift. A sign lists THERAPIES, BLOOD TESTS, WAITING AREA B and TOILETS.
  • Waiting area B First floor
    Look for: An area with green chairs along the walls. The sign says WAITING AREA B. Rooms open off this area.
