Share

Share what a place is like

Anyone can share photos and short videos of a place, so that other people can see what it is like before they go. You might be a visitor, a family member, a member of staff or the owner. You do not need an account, you do not need to give a diagnosis and there is nothing to explain.

This is one part of iSEE, called What it's like. The other two parts are Places and Find your way.

Please read the ground rules below, then use the button at the end of this page. No photos or videos have been published yet.

What helps

Photos and short videos of any part of a place help. These are the parts people most often want to see first:

  • The way in
  • The doors
  • The counter or reception
  • Where you wait
  • The toilets
  • The lift and the stairs
  • The lighting and the sound
  • The seating
  • Parking and drop-off

Ground rules

  • Share photos and videos only of places you are allowed to photograph.
  • Photograph the place, not the people. Keep out faces, children and number plates.
  • Keep out screens and paperwork that show private details.
  • Say what the photo or video shows and when it was taken.
  • You keep ownership of what you share. You give Autism & Me permission to show it on iSEE.
  • You can ask for it to be taken down at any time.
  • Autism & Me looks at everything before it appears, so nothing shows straight away.

What is collected

  • The photo or video files you add.
  • The place name and the county.
  • An email address, only if you want a reply. It is optional.

These are stored with Wix, for Autism & Me. The privacy page explains more, including how to ask for something to be removed.

To ask for something to be taken down, email contactautismandme@gmail.com. You do not need to share any personal details.
