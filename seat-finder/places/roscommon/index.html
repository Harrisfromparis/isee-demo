    About this list

    Place names and addresses come from OpenStreetMap. © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence. The licence is described at openstreetmap.org/copyright.

    List generated: the date will appear here once the list has loaded.

    Not every place in Ireland is in this list, and an Eircode is shown only where the open data has one.

    Being listed here does not mean a place has an iSEE guide or has been checked.

    This list covers the 26 counties that use Eircodes. Northern Ireland is not included.

    To correct or remove a listing, tell us. Email contactautismandme@gmail.com. You do not need to share any personal details.
