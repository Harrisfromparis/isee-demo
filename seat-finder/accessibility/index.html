For everyone

Designed for everyone

iSEE is built so anyone can use it in the way that suits them. There is nothing to declare and nothing to switch on. Every way of using it is open to everyone.

This page describes those ways, what we aim for, and what has and has not been tested.

Ways to use iSEE

  • Read it or hear it. Every route step is written as text. A map is optional. A step is read aloud only when you ask. Nothing speaks or plays by itself.
  • Step by step or as an overview. See one step at a time, or the whole route at once. Go back, repeat a step, or say you are somewhere else.
  • With or without stairs. Choose the standard route, the shortest way, or the step-free route, which never uses stairs or paths that have not been checked.
  • Keyboard, touch, mouse or screen reader. Everything works with the keyboard. The focus ring is a two-colour outline that shows on light and dark backgrounds. Pages use headings, landmarks and lists, and there is a skip link on every page.
  • Your own size and pace. Text is 18 pixels by default, never below 16, and scales if you change your browser's text size. Pages reflow down to 320 CSS pixels wide with no sideways scrolling. Buttons and links are at least 44 pixels high. Nothing is timed and nothing moves on by itself.
  • Calm by design. There is no flashing, no autoplay and no parallax. If your device asks for reduced motion, the site uses none. Meaning is never carried by colour alone: every status has an icon and a word.
  • Descriptions and captions. Photos and videos that people share will have a description added, and videos will have captions, before Autism & Me publishes them. None have been published yet.
  • With or without JavaScript. Every page makes sense with JavaScript turned off. The route planner and the places list are the parts that need it.

Our target

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA. This is a target. It is not a certification, and nobody outside Autism & Me has audited this website.

What has and has not been tested

  • Not yet tested by people using screen readers, on phones or in a venue.
  • Checks so far are automated checks and our own review. Automated checks find only some problems.
  • The only venue is a made-up demo. No real venue data has been checked.
  • Maps are optional. If a map is shown, the written directions are the version to rely on.

Tell us what works

If something on this website does not work as you would expect, email contactautismandme@gmail.com. You do not need to share any personal details.
